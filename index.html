How We Handle Mold
Across Florida

Florida’s humidity and storms make mold a hidden, persistent problem. Our step-by-step process of inspection, containment, removal, and prevention ensures lasting results.

1
Detailed Property Assessment Through Certified Mold Inspection

We locate hidden mold sources, test air quality, and provide a clear action plan to fix the problem at its root.

2
Controlled Containment to Stop Mold Spread

We set up barriers, negative air pressure, and filtration to prevent spores from spreading to clean areas.

3
Safe Removal of Contaminated Materials

Damaged drywall, wood, insulation, and flooring are removed, treated, and cleaned to stop further contamination.

4
Long-Term Moisture Control and Prevention

We recommend ventilation upgrades, humidity control, and water repairs to reduce future mold risk.
