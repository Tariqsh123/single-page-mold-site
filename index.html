Our Proven Mold Remediation Process in Orlando

We follow proven steps based on EPA mold remediation guidelines:

1
Inspection and Assessment

We start with a detailed inspection of your property in Orlando, checking for visible mold, hidden growth, moisture issues, and water-damaged materials. We carefully examine attics, crawl spaces, basements, and other high-risk areas to find the true source of the problem.

2
Containment

To stop mold from spreading, we seal off the affected areas using professional containment barriers. Negative air machines and HEPA air scrubbers capture airborne spores and help protect the rest of your home or business during cleanup.

3
Safe Removal

We remove contaminated drywall, insulation, carpet, and other porous materials following strict industry safety standards. Our technicians wear protective equipment, including respirators, to ensure safe and controlled removal.

4
Cleaning and Treatment

After removing damaged materials, we clean and treat all affected surfaces to eliminate mold at its source. We also dry structural areas such as walls and flooring to remove trapped moisture and lower the risk of regrowth.

5
Drying and Prevention

Because Central Florida’s high humidity creates ideal conditions for mold, we use professional drying equipment and may recommend dehumidification to maintain safe moisture levels and help prevent future growth.

6
Post-Remediation Verification

Once the work is complete, we perform clearance testing to confirm that humidity levels are safe and indoor air quality has been properly restored.
